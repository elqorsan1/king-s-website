About KING'S

KING'S is a Stronghold Crusader clan built around organized clan-versus-clan matches on GameRanger, not random public lobbies. Every player trains the same way: studying army timing, economy build orders, and castle layouts that hold up against a real siege instead of just an AI.

The roster is split into three levels: Academy, Medium, and Expert. That way newer players learn against opponents their own size instead of getting thrown straight into a clan war, and moving up a level is how a player earns a permanent spot in the lineup.

Iron gates. Steady hands. No surrender.

The King's Code

Six rules keep a garrison standing. Every member is expected to know them before their first clan match.

  1. I.

    Respect the Garrison

    Treat every member and every opponent with respect, whether you're holding the castle or losing it.

  2. II.

    Honor the Muster

    If you commit to a clan match, show up on time, or tell leadership in advance that you can't.

  3. III.

    Fight Fair

    No cheats, exploits, map-hacks, or deliberate desync abuse. A win earned that way isn't a win.

  4. IV.

    Wear the Crest with Pride

    In public lobbies you represent KING'S whether you mean to or not, so play like it.

  5. V.

    Hold the Line Together

    Talk during clan wars. Silence loses castles faster than any siege engine.

  6. VI.

    One Banner

    Playing under another clan's tag during an active war needs leadership's approval first.
